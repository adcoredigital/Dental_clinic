OUR SPECIALITIES

Complete Dental Care Under One Roof

Explore general information about dental services. Availability and suitability must be confirmed with the clinic.

General Dentistry

Routine dental examinations, oral health assessments, fillings, and preventive care.

Teeth Cleaning & Scaling

Professional cleaning to help remove plaque and tartar and support healthy gums.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Smile-enhancement treatments designed around individual needs and expectations.

Teeth Whitening

Professional assessment and suitable whitening options for a brighter smile.

Root Canal Treatment

Treatment for infected or damaged dental pulp, aimed at relieving discomfort and preserving the tooth when appropriate.

Dental Implants

Assessment and tooth-replacement options for suitable candidates.

Orthodontics & Braces

Evaluation and treatment options to correct tooth alignment and bite concerns.

Clear Aligners

Removable orthodontic appliances for eligible patients seeking an alternative to traditional braces.

Crowns & Bridges

Restorative solutions to repair damaged teeth or replace missing teeth.

Dentures

Options for replacing multiple missing teeth or a complete set of teeth.

Paediatric Dentistry

Gentle, age-appropriate dental care for children.

Gum Care & Periodontics

Assessment and management of gum disease and supporting oral tissues.

Wisdom Tooth Assessment

Evaluation of wisdom teeth and advice on appropriate treatment where necessary.

Emergency Dental Care

Assessment of urgent dental concerns, subject to clinic availability.
